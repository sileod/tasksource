The initial pilot produced no eligibility passes. The follow-up prioritized explicit
resources and dataset/corpus/benchmark-style titles: 16 such leads remained after
the pilot, with seven clear non-English titles held aside. Multilingual leads were
retained for actual English training-data verification. Other leads remain unreviewed,
not rejected. Full matching reports retain the held sources and overlap evidence.

Two follow-up requests exceeded the reviewer input limit. Both succeeded after a
bounded-evidence retry; original failures remain in the review history. Excerpts
are explicitly marked truncated and complete source responses remain cached.

## Reproduction and scope

See the [discovery commands](../../src/tasksource/task_discovery/README.md#hugging-face-papers)
and [source implementation](../../src/tasksource/task_discovery/hf_papers.py).
The five exact default queries are defined in that source. Cached metadata includes
search results, native abstracts, GitHub/project links and repositories explicitly
tagged with each paper’s arXiv ID; linked resources are not assumed to be official.

Native Jev reviews check training availability, decision fit, actual dataset language
and annotation provenance. Exact prior paper IDs now suppress repeated paper leads;
distinct dataset records sharing a paper remain separate. Catalog overlap hints are
held for identity review with `--hold-related`. This is a conservative work-saving
policy, not a claim that every possible annotation of a related source exists.

[Machine-readable results](2026-10-13-hf-papers.jsonl) retain judgments and review
hashes without raw dataset rows or abstract dumps. Local manifests and commands
under `build/discovery/hf-papers/` record exclusions, prefilter rules, inputs and
source-response checksums. The [prospects index](../prospects.md) keeps other passes separate.
