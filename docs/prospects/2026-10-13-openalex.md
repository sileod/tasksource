# OpenAlex discovery passes — October 13, 2026

Bounded general, medical and legal searches for English decision workloads.
These are discovery results only: no datasets were implemented or repackaged.

| Domain | Retrieved paper leads | Pilot reviews | Resource-linked follow-up reviews | Final English shortlist |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| General | 382 | 12 | 13 | 0 |
| Medical | 382 | 12 | 14 | 0 |
| Legal | 320 | 12 | 10 | 0 |

Inventories can overlap across domains; the review passes exclude earlier selections.
There were 73 distinct reviewed paper leads and two additional language rechecks.
All 36 pilot reviews lacked sufficient eligibility evidence. The 37 follow-up
reviews prioritized explicit HF/GitHub distributions rather than spending on
hundreds of abstract-only records.

Two general-pass sources initially passed eligibility/provenance checks:

- [AMuRD](https://github.com/Update-For-Integrated-Business-AI/AMuRD): Arabic–English receipt annotations; retained as unresolved because the English training-language evidence did not reach the screening threshold.
- [T2Ranking](https://github.com/THUIR/T2Ranking): Chinese passage ranking; unsuitable for this English-scoped pass, not a general dataset rejection.

Neither enters the final English shortlist. Missing evidence is not evidence of
bad data. These search results also contain method papers, already-covered datasets
under paper titles and non-English datasets described in English papers.
The empty handoff does not establish that these domains lack worthwhile additions.

## Reproduction and limitations

Run [scripts/discover_openalex.py](../../scripts/discover_openalex.py), first with
`--pilot-only`, then without it after inspecting the pilot. The script defines four
queries per domain, requests up to 100 papers per query, and saves source responses,
commands, matching results, reviewer controls and per-model judgments. Discovery
uses native Jev, prior-screening exclusions and the provenance policy documented
in the [discovery tools](../../src/tasksource/task_discovery/README.md).

OpenAlex language filtering applies to papers. A separate review criterion now
requires evidence about the actual training-data language; metadata language alone
does not suffice. Abstracts are reconstructed and retained rather than discarded.
Train/resource evidence remains the main limitation of abstract-first discovery.

The local run artifacts remain under `build/discovery/openalex-en/`; this report
summarizes the completed pass without publishing raw source abstracts or dataset rows.
The [existing prospects](../prospects.md) remain separate and unchanged.
